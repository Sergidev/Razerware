About Razerware

Razerware is a portfolio project. It is a demo e-commerce site built to showcase full stack development skills, including an AI shopping advisor.

No real sales take place on this website. Nothing can be bought, no payments are processed, and no products will ever be shipped. The checkout is a simulation.

All brands, product names, models and prices shown in the catalog are fictional. Any resemblance to real brands, products or companies is purely coincidental. Names of real hardware components, such as processors or graphics cards, belong to their respective owners and are used for illustration only. Product images were generated with AI.

This project was designed and developed by Sergi Villalobos Gascón.
